About AudiFriends

## Overview

AudiFriends is a social platform for audiobook enthusiasts. We connect people through their shared love of audiobooks, using the Spotify API to power our audiobook discovery features.

## Features
search

Search millions of audiobooks via Spotify integration. Find your next listen in seconds.

library

Build your personal audiobook collection. Save favorites and track what you've listened to.

friends

Connect with people who share your taste. See what friends are listening to and discover through them.

ai_recommend

Get personalized recommendations based on your library and listening preferences.

## Tech Stack

## Privacy

Your data is stored securely. You control what you share and who can see your library. We never sell your information.
